# Sources and third-party credits

Mendo's project authorship is attributed to EQ Studio. This does not mean every
dependency, component, algorithm or earlier mechanism was created by EQ Studio.

- [Espressif ESP-IDF](https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf), version 5.5.4:
  installed separately; retain its Apache-2.0 and component-specific notices.
- ESP-SR, ESP Codec Dev, ESP WebSocket Client, ESP-DSP and Opus dependencies:
  resolved by the dependency manifests and lockfile. Their upstream notices and
  licenses remain applicable; SDK and managed-component caches are not bundled.
- [Xiaozhi ESP32](https://github.com/78/xiaozhi-esp32): implementation reference
  and service ecosystem. Xiaozhi service accounts and voice rights are separate.
- [Shaztech/Pilldispenser](https://github.com/Shaztech/Pilldispenser): an early
  mechanism and software reference. Original downloaded sample files are not
  included. Do not claim Mendo invented pill dispensers or their general concept.
- ALIENTEK DNESP32S3 vendor documentation and examples informed board mapping.
  Vendor PDFs, example archives and branding are not redistributed.
- [OpenSCAD MCAD](https://github.com/openscad/MCAD), used by gear CAD; install
  separately with OpenSCAD and retain its own notices for the library.

Generated neutral tones in this candidate are mathematical sine signals, not
family voice clones or licensed songs. The selected photograph is supplied by
the project owner and re-encoded without camera metadata.

A complete line-by-line third-party provenance review is still pending. This
notice is an inventory of known sources, not a claim that attribution alone
replaces permission. Unclear assets should stay out of a public release until
their provenance and terms are resolved.
